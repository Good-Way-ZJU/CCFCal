CCFCal Release

CCFCal v1.0.2

A focused macOS menu bar countdown for the CS and AI venues you want to track.

Highlights

  • See the nearest target directly in the menu bar, such as AAAI 1d.
  • Browse CS and AI venues by CCF-A/B/C/NONE, domain, and keyword.
  • Track abstract registration and full-paper deadlines.
  • Sync only your selected deadlines to a dedicated Calendar collection.

Data

  • Candidate data is generated from the ccfddl source.
  • Installed apps refresh the hosted candidate snapshot at most once per day on launch.
  • Deadline timestamps are displayed in the Mac's local time zone.

Install

  • Download and unzip CCFCal.
  • Move CCFCal.app to Applications.
  • Grant Calendar access when prompted.
